📖 Game Guide & Controls

Movement & Exploration Use WASD or Arrow Keys to walk and strafe. Use 4 and 6 to turn smoothly.
Combat & Magic Press Space, Enter, or 5 to swing your weapon. Press 7 to unleash your active spell.
Menus & Journal Press Q (or F1) for the main system menu. Press E (or F2 / #) to view your inventory and equip items.
Maps & Rest Press 1 to open the regional map and quest log. Press 9 to rest and regain health/magicka.

💾 Save Data Management

Progress is saved into your browser's persistent storage (IndexedDB) whenever you choose Save Game in the in-game menu. Export a JSON backup to protect against browser cache clearing or to transfer your character to another computer.
