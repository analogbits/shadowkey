⚡ Developer Cheat Menu

Press esc (or Q) to open the in-game menu during gameplay and select Cheat Menu:

  • God Mode: Toggle complete invulnerability
  • Ghost Mode: Toggle noclip (walk through walls)
  • Gold & EXP: +5,000 Gold, +1,000 EXP, Instant Level Up
  • Merchants: Open any weapon, armor, spell, or item shop
  • Teleport: Instant jump to any dungeon, crypt, or town
  • Keys & Artifacts: All 11 Shadowkeys & Star Teeth

💾 Save Data

Progress is saved to your browser's IndexedDB when you choose Save Game in the in-game menu. Export a backup before clearing Safari/Chrome data.
