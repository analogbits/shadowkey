# The Elder Scrolls Travels: Shadowkey (Web & Mobile Edition)

Play **The Elder Scrolls Travels: Shadowkey** (2004, Nokia N-Gage) directly in modern web browsers, on Mac/PC, and on iOS / Android mobile devices via WebAssembly.

Hosted online on GitHub Pages: **[https://analogbits.github.io/shadowkey/](https://analogbits.github.io/shadowkey/)**

---

## 📱 Mobile & iOS Experience

- **Dual-Thumb Virtual Gamepad:** Ergonomic touch controls designed specifically for handheld mobile play. Left thumb controls walk & strafe D-Pad; right thumb controls camera turning (`↺ Turn L`, `↻ Turn R`), combat (`⚔️ Attack`), spells (`✨ Spell`), and camera tilt (`👁️ Look`).
- **Quick-Access Utility Bar:** One-tap mobile shortcuts for System Menu, Inventory, Map & Quest Journal, Character Stats, and Resting.
- **Landscape Handheld Console Mode:** Rotate your phone to landscape for an authentic handheld gaming experience with controls floating neatly on thumb rest positions.
- **True Multi-Touch:** Walk forward while simultaneously attacking, casting, or turning without stuck keys or dropped inputs.
- **iOS Safari & PWA Ready:** Add to Home Screen on iPhone/iPad to play fullscreen without browser address bars; includes iOS safe area support for iPhone notch & Dynamic Island.
- **iOS WebAudio Auto-Unlock:** Resumes audio playback instantly on initial touch gesture.

## ⚡ Developer Cheat Menu

The developer cheat menu built into the original Vir2L engine has been unlocked.

During gameplay, press <kbd>Esc</kbd> or <kbd>Q</kbd> (or click the **Menu** button) to open the in-game options menu, then choose **Cheat Menu**:

- **God Mode:** Toggle complete invulnerability on/off.
- **Ghost Mode:** Toggle noclip mode to walk through walls and gates.
- **Gold & Experience:** Instantly give +5,000 Gold, +1,000 EXP, or perform an instant Level Up.
- **Universal Merchants:** Access any vendor anywhere (Armor, Weapons, Spells, Items, Shields).
- **All Shadowkeys & Star Teeth:** Instantly obtain all 11 Shadowkeys and 7 Star Teeth quest items.
- **Teleportation:** Teleport immediately to any town, dungeon, or crypt in Hammerfell/High Rock (Azra's Crossing, Delfhide, Earthtear Caverns, Ghast Pass, Dragonstar West/East, Crypt of Hearts, etc.).
- **Super Blaze & Heal Wounds:** Max out magicka and learn top-tier spells.

## Display & Upscaling Presets

- **2x (352×416):** Compact integer scale.
- **3x (528×624):** Default balanced high-resolution integer scale for laptops and desktop monitors.
- **4x (704×832):** Maximum high-res crisp integer presentation.
- **Fit:** Proportional aspect-ratio fit matching browser window dimensions.
- **Sharp vs. LCD vs. Smooth:** Switch between pixel-perfect nearest neighbor, authentic backlit LCD matrix scanlines, or smoothed bilinear filtering.
